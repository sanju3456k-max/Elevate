About Elevate Labs

An educational mock-test practice, OMR evaluation, batch learning, and study analytics platform for students and coaching institutes.

Timed Mock Tests

Chapter-wise and full-length practice exams with instant scoring, detailed solutions, and performance analytics.

Batch Lectures & PDFs

Structured coaching courses, video classes, study notes, and printable OMR sheets in one clean workspace.

Multi-Institute Isolation

Each partner coaching institute runs on its own isolated cloud project with strict access control.

How Google Sign-In Is Used

Elevate Labs uses Google Sign-In (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile) strictly to authenticate students and institute administrators, sync test progress and study notes across devices, and unlock enrolled institute batches. We do not collect or process any online payments inside the app.

Platforms Supported

  • Web & PWA: Accessible on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers at labselevate.vercel.app.
  • Android Apps: Dedicated signed Android applications for Elevate and white-label partner institutes.
