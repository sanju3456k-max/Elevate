Privacy Policy

Elevate Labs (and authorized white-label coaching institute apps) · Last Updated: November 26, 2026

Elevate Labs ("we", "our", or "us") provides an online mock-test, batch learning, and study analytics platform via our web application (labselevate.vercel.app) and official Android applications (including isolated white-label institute apps such as Apex Academy). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how you can manage or delete your data.

1. Information We Collect

  • Google Sign-In Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic profile details through Google OAuth (openid, userinfo.email, userinfo.profile): your name, email address, profile photo URL, and unique account identifier. We never access or store your Google password.
  • Learning & Assessment Data: Mock tests you create or attempt, answers, scores, time analytics, bookmarks, study notes, and batch enrollments.
  • Session & Device Security Metadata: A generated app device identifier and basic device model/platform label to enforce single-device account security and prevent unauthorized account sharing.
  • Optional User-Triggered Media & Hardware Access: On Android, Camera, Microphone, or Media/File access is used only when you explicitly choose to upload an image/document or use a voice or scan feature. Push notification permission is used solely for lecture, test, and batch announcements.
  • Guest Mode: If you use Guest Mode without signing in, your activity stays locally on your device and is not linked to a Google account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and sync your tests, notes, and batch progress across sessions.
  • Display personalized performance analytics, leaderboards, and institute batch content.
  • Route enrolled students on the web portal to their respective institute's dedicated app using a one-way SHA-256 hash of their email address.
  • Maintain platform security, prevent abuse, and send optional study/batch notifications.

3. Payments & Financial Information

No In-App Payment Processing: Elevate Labs does not integrate an online payment gateway inside the app and does not collect, process, or store credit/debit card numbers, bank account details, or UPI PINs/credentials. Any batch pricing displayed is informational; fee collection and batch enrollment are handled directly by the respective coaching institute.

4. Data Storage, Isolation & Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Authentication and cloud database storage are powered by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) protected by strict security rules.
  • Multi-Institute Isolation: Each white-label institute application operates on its own isolated Firebase project, keeping institute data separate.
  • AI Educational Features: When you use optional AI-assisted question or explanation tools, only the submitted question/prompt text is processed to generate educational output.
  • No Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your learning data only as long as your account remains active or as needed by your enrolled institute. You may request complete deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by emailing sanju3456k@gmail.com from your registered email address (or contacting your respective institute administrator). Deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

6. Children's Privacy

Our platform is designed for exam preparation and coaching students. If a parent or guardian believes a minor has provided personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us to have the data removed.

7. Updates & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy whenever new features, permissions, or services are added to the platform. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to sanju3456k@gmail.com.
